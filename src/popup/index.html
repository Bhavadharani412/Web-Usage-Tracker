Welcome aboard!

Get started in four quick steps.

  1. 1
    Load unpacked
    Go to chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, then click Load unpacked and select the web-usage-tracker folder.
  2. 2
    Pin WebTrack
    Click the puzzle icon in the Chrome toolbar and pin WebTrack for one-click access.
  3. 3
    Browse normally
    WebTrack tracks time automatically. It pauses when you're idle or switch to another app — no setup needed.
  4. 4
    Open the popup
    Click the WebTrack icon to see today's active web time. Hit View weekly report for deeper insights.
🔒 Privacy-first. Your data never leaves your device — no server, no account, no analytics.
